Other styles

Made on one day, 26 September 2026, after being asked to try the trip's subjects in styles other than watercolour: a linocut, a roadside postcard, pointillism, pen and ink, cross-stitch, Van Gogh, a fruit-crate label. All of them are made in code on the same kit as the paintings, with the watercolour filters switched off, so none of them are the real medium. They are pastiches, and the notes say which master each one is after and how far short it falls.
